description=[h1]LX Heli Pack Split-Screen Fix[/h1]
description=Splitscreen / split screen / local couch co-op fix for the Heli Pack mod (Black Hawk, Apache, Little Bird, EC135, Kiowa) in Project Zomboid Build 42 (B42), plus the gamepad support it never had at all. Player 2 can finally fly.
description=Heli Pack is five copies of the same code and 81% of its player lookups are pinned to player 1. Fixing that turned out to need 38 changes, not four hundred.
description=
description=[h2]What it does[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Player 2 could not fly at all[/b] - The mod asks whether PLAYER 0 is sitting in an aircraft.
description=[*][b]And your helicopter was flown with the other player's controls[/b] - This one is worse than being locked out.
description=[*][b]Both sticks now fly, and better than the keyboard[/b] - There was no controller support whatsoever: zero JoypadState and zero getJoypadData across 42269 lines.
description=[*][b]The layout is yours to choose[/b] - Default is how a real helicopter works: left stick climbs and turns, right stick is the cyclic. One setting swaps it for what you expect coming from a car.
description=[*][b]Five helicopters, one set of patches[/b] - Heli Pack is five copies of the same code, 87% to 98% identical, so every patch was written once and verified separately against all five.
description=[*][b]Nothing of Heli Pack is redistributed[/b] - Its files are read from your installed copy at load time, patched in memory and run from there.
description=[/list]
description=[b]Requires:[/b] [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3822141121]Heli Pack[/url]
description=[b]Load it AFTER[/b] Heli Pack.
description=[b]Install:[/b] Subscribe, then enable it under Mods in the main menu. In split-screen, enabling it once covers every player.
description=[b]Options:[/b] Main menu > Options > MODS > "LX Heli Pack Split": stick layout (helicopter or game), dead zone, and inverted vertical axis. The split-screen fixes are always on and have nothing to configure.
